# GestureHeal — Demo Script

---

## 🎬 Demo Overview

**Duration**: 3-5 minutes  
**Goal**: Show real-time gesture recognition triggering medical actions

---

## Pre-Demo Setup

1. ✅ Open browser to `http://localhost:5173`
2. ✅ Ensure camera permissions granted
3. ✅ Good lighting on hands
4. ✅ Practice gestures beforehand
5. ✅ Have backup screen recording ready

---

## Script Flow

---

### 🎤 [0:00-0:30] Introduction

**SAY:**
> "Imagine you're in a hospital, unable to speak. You're in pain, you need help, but you can't type fast enough. What do you do?"
>
> "GestureHeal solves this. It's an AI system that reads your hand gestures and triggers medical actions instantly."

**SHOW:** Home screen with GestureHeal logo

---

### 🔐 [0:30-1:00] Login Flow

**SAY:**
> "Let me show you how it works. I'll log in as a patient."

**DO:**
1. Select "Patient" tab
2. Enter any email/password
3. Click "Login"

**RESULT:** Patient Dashboard appears

---

### 📋 [1:00-1:30] Patient Dashboard

**SAY:**
> "This is the patient dashboard. I can see my care team and book appointments with my doctors."

**DO:**
1. Point to "Your Care Team" section
2. Click "Book Appointment" on one doctor card

**SAY:**
> "But the key feature is this..."

**POINT:** Big blue "Start Emergency Communication" card

---

### 📷 [1:30-2:30] Camera View & Gesture Detection

**SAY:**
> "When I click this, my camera activates and the AI starts watching my hands."

**DO:**
1. Click "Start Emergency Communication"
2. Camera view opens (simulated in demo)

**SAY:**
> "Now, the system is running MediaPipe to detect my hand landmarks, then our ML model classifies the gesture."

**DO:**
1. Hold up hand
2. Make a gesture (e.g., HELP gesture)

**SAY:**
> "Watch — it detected my gesture and shows it below the camera feed."

---

### 🚨 [2:30-3:30] Emergency Alert Flow

**SAY:**
> "But in a real emergency, the patient might not wait for detection. They can tap this emergency button."

**DO:**
1. Click red Emergency button
2. Emergency grid appears

**SAY:**
> "They select their problem — CHEST PAIN, CAN'T BREATHE, HELP — whatever fits."

**DO:**
1. Click "CHEST PAIN"

**RESULT:** Red "EMERGENCY ALERT SENT" overlay appears

**SAY:**
> "Instantly, the doctor is notified. No typing, no speaking, just one tap."

---

### 🔔 [3:30-4:00] Notification System

**SAY:**
> "The patient can also check for doctor responses using this notification bell."

**DO:**
1. Dismiss the alert
2. Click notification bell icon

**SAY:**
> "When our backend is connected, doctor messages will appear here in real-time."

---

### 🎯 [4:00-4:30] Summary

**SAY:**
> "To summarize:
> - Real-time gesture detection
> - Emergency communication without speech or typing
> - Doctor notification system
> - Appointment booking
> - All running in the browser, no app install needed."

---

### 💡 [4:30-5:00] Closing

**SAY:**
> "GestureHeal makes healthcare accessible for the 6.3 million deaf Indians and millions more who struggle to communicate during medical emergencies."
>
> "Every hand gesture can save a life."

**END**

---

## Backup Plan

If live demo fails:
1. Switch to screen recording backup
2. Explain: "Due to camera issues, here's a recording of the system in action"
3. Continue with same script

---

## Tips

- Make gestures SLOW and CLEAR for camera
- Keep explaining while waiting for detection
- If something breaks, acknowledge and move on
- Confidence > perfection
